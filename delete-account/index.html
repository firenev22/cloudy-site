Deleting a Cloudy profile and its data

Applies to Cloudy and Cloudy Server · CloudyServerDev@gmail.com

There is no Cloudy account, and we hold no data to delete. Cloudy has no sign-up, no servers of ours, and nothing is uploaded anywhere. A "profile" is a name kept on the Cloudy Server that you or somebody you know is running, on their own phone or PC. Deleting it takes a few seconds in the app, and nothing needs to be requested from us.

Delete one profile

On any device signed in to that server:

  • Open Cloudy and go to Settings.
  • Tap the person's name, then Remove, and confirm.

This deletes that profile and everything kept for it: playback history, resume positions, likes and playlists. An administrator can remove anybody; the last administrator cannot be removed, because that would lock everyone out of the server.

Delete everything

Everything Cloudy stores lives on the devices you own, so uninstalling removes it:

  • On a phone or tablet running Cloudy (the player): uninstalling deletes its pairing, its settings and any downloads kept for offline playback.
  • On the device running Cloudy Server: uninstalling, or Android Settings → Apps → Cloudy Server → Storage → Clear storage, deletes the library index, every profile and its history, all pairings, saved artwork and any converted copies.

What is kept

Your own media files are never touched. Cloudy reads the music and videos where they already are and writes nothing over them, so removing a profile or the app itself leaves your files exactly as they were. Deleting a profile does not delete media, and neither does uninstalling.

What we keep

Nothing. We operate no servers that your library, your profiles or your viewing history pass through, so there is no account for us to close and no retention period to wait out. The privacy policy lists the few services the apps contact and what each one receives.

If you cannot reach the server

A profile lives on the server device. If you no longer have access to it, ask whoever runs that server to remove your profile, or have them revoke your device under Settings, which cuts that device off from the library immediately. If you need help, write to CloudyServerDev@gmail.com.
