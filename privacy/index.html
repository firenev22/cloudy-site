Privacy Policy

Effective 17 September 2026 · Published by Cloudy · CloudyServerDev@gmail.com
Applies to Cloudy (Music, Theatre and the TV app), Cloudy Server and Cloudy Server Pro on Android, and Cloudy for Windows.

The short version. Cloudy plays your own music and videos from a server you run yourself, on your own phone or PC. We don't run any servers that your library, your viewing history or your account passes through. We have no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your data stays on your devices unless you turn on a feature that connects to one of the services listed below.

What Cloudy stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your own devices: the device running Cloudy Server, and the devices you pair with it. We never receive any of it.

DataWhere it livesWhy
Your library index: file names, folders, durations, tags, artwork, descriptionsThe server deviceTo browse and play your media
Profiles: names, optional photos, colours, optional passwordsThe server device. Passwords are stored only as salted PBKDF2 hashes, never in plain text.So each person in your household has their own history and settings
Playback history, resume positions, likes, playlistsThe server deviceTo resume and personalise playback
Paired devicesThe device name and a revocable access token on the server; the token on each paired deviceSo only devices you invited can reach your library
Downloads for offline playbackThe device that downloaded themTo play without a connection
Uploaded filesThe server device, in your library foldersBecause you chose to upload them
Crash reportsThe server device onlyTo show you what went wrong. They are never sent to us.

Deleting the app, or clearing its storage, removes all of this from that device. An admin can revoke any paired device from the server at any time. See deleting a profile and its data for the exact steps.

Permissions Cloudy asks for

  • Music, video and image access: to find and serve your media, and the posters stored next to it.
  • Folder access (the folders you choose): to read, organise, convert and delete files in your library when you ask it to.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls and that the server is running.
  • Network access: so your devices can reach your server.
  • Running in the background: so the server keeps serving while the screen is off.

Services Cloudy contacts

Cloudy connects to the services below only for the purpose shown. When it does, those services receive your device's IP address and the request itself, and their own privacy policies apply.

ServiceWhenWhat is sent
TVmazeWhen the server looks up TV showsShow names and episode numbers from your library
MusicBrainzWhen the server looks up music detailsArtist and album names
The Movie Database (TMDB)Only if you add your own TMDB keyFilm and show names
OpenSubtitlesOnly if you add your own OpenSubtitles key and search for subtitlesThe film or episode title, and your key
TailscaleOnly if you set up remote access with your own Tailscale keyWhat Tailscale needs to connect your devices. Cloudy turns off Tailscale's own logging, and your media travels encrypted, directly between your devices where possible.
CloudflareOnly while you use a temporary invite or a web shareThe traffic of that share, which passes through Cloudflare's network encrypted
Google Play services code scannerWhen you scan a pairing code with the cameraThe image is processed on your device. Google may collect diagnostic and usage information about the scanner, under Google's privacy policy.
Google Play BillingOnly when you buy or restore Cloudy Server ProPurchase details, handled by Google. We receive confirmation of the purchase, never your payment details.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share personal data, because we don't collect it. Web shares you create are links to specific items: anyone with the link can open that item until the share expires or you delete it, so only send one to people you mean to.

Children

Cloudy is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone, children included.

Security

Every request to your server needs a token issued at pairing, and tokens can be revoked individually. Remote access through Tailscale or Cloudflare is encrypted. Cloudy never opens your server to the internet unless you turn on remote access or a web share.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and note the change in the app's release notes.
